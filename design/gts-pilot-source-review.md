# GTS 125 pilot — source and scope review

Review date: 2026-10-30. This is a source-reading review, not an independent mechanic or manufacturer approval. Page references below are 1-based PDF page numbers. Full source PDFs remain private; source scan/image reproduction rights are not established.

## Identity
- `GTS125 Parts Diagram.pdf`, PDF p. 2: visually states “Vespa GTS 125 4T (Euro 3) (UK)”, model `ZAPM31300`. Catalogue date, chassis revision boundaries and the actual owner's machine have not been reconciled.
- `GTS125 Workshop Manual.pdf`, PDF pp. 1–3: service station manual GTS 125, copyright 2007, professional audience. PDF p. 9 gives a chassis-prefix notation and engine prefix, but its range notation requires reconciliation. Do not infer model year, all markets or a serial range from a photograph.
- `GTS125 Owners Guide Manual.pdf`, PDF p. 18: describes chassis/engine identification and the need for identification numbers for spare part requests. Owner's guide edition/market applicability remains unconfirmed. A family/displacement selector is not sufficient to verify the owner’s scooter.
- `GTS125ie Super Workshop Manual.pdf`, cover: separate GTS Super 125 i.e. (2009) reference; no assumed cross-application.

## Action review
- **Mirror:** Owner's guide PDF p. 54 mentions removing mirrors within a headlamp-access sequence. Workshop manual PDF p. 266 lists mirror replacement operation 004066. The latter is an operation code, not a part number. Neither page is a complete verified mirror replacement method. The pilot therefore presents an informational note only; it asserts no tightening direction, thread type, torque, replacement part or fitment.
- **Front tyre:** Owner's guide PDF pp. 24 and 39–40 describe periodic wear/pressure checks with cold tyres; PDF p. 60 refers tyre replacement to an equipped service provider. Do not infer pressure, internal tyre condition or roadworthiness from a photo. No vehicle-specific pressure numbers are displayed until exact applicability is verified. The vehicle manufacturer's recommendation is the reference, not a sidewall maximum.
- **Visual references:** `gts125-reference-study.png` and its edited background plates are AI-generated interpretive studies. Mirror and tyre geometry, perspective and hidden surfaces have not been checked for dimensional accuracy. The procedural 3D model is an original approximate study, not a factory part or manufactured mechanical reconstruction. No catalogue diagram is redistributed.
